Electricity Flows Where Wheels Go

Harnessing vehicle kinetic energy through innovative rack and pinion speed breaker mechanisms to provide decentralized power generation.

Explore Technology

Project Overview

The Problem

Conventional power generation relies heavily on fossil fuels, contributing to air pollution and environmental degradation. Concurrently, kinetic energy from heavy vehicular traffic on speed breakers is continuously lost as heat and vibration.

The Solution

PulseRoads converts vertical displacement caused by passing vehicles into rotational energy using an embedded rack and pinion assembly, driving low-RPM dynamos to output continuous clean electricity.

Key Impact

A modular, retrofittable system designed to power street lights, EV charging stations, and rural off-grid areas without consuming fuel.

Core Mechanical Design

1. Speed Breaker Platform

Functions as the upper interface, depressing vertically under the force of vehicle weight to initiate motion.

2. Linear Rack & Pinion

Transfers downward linear motion to the linear rack, engaging with the pinion gear to produce smooth rotation.

3. Return Springs

Spring-loaded assemblies restore the platform and rack to initial positions after each vehicle pass for ongoing operation.

4. Generator (Dynamo)

Converts rotational shaft movement into electrical energy via electromagnetic induction.

5. Power Management

Utilizes rectifiers, voltage regulators, and storage units (batteries/supercapacitors) to stabilize grid-ready output.

6. Smart Maintenance

Designed for integration with IoT monitoring for predictive maintenance and alignment checks under heavy traffic.
