# ⚡ PulseRoads

### Electricity Flows Where Wheels Go

**PulseRoads** is an innovative kinetic energy harvesting system that converts the vertical movement produced by vehicles passing over a specially designed speed breaker into rotational mechanical energy and, ultimately, electrical energy.

## 🚀 Overview

A significant amount of kinetic energy generated by moving vehicles is normally lost as heat, vibration, and mechanical deformation when vehicles pass over speed breakers.

PulseRoads aims to capture a portion of this otherwise wasted energy.

The system uses an **embedded rack-and-pinion mechanism** to convert the vertical displacement of a speed-breaker platform into rotational motion. This rotational motion drives a **low-RPM dynamo**, which generates electrical energy.

The generated electricity can then be regulated and stored using power-management components such as rectifiers, voltage regulators, batteries, and supercapacitors.

The basic energy-conversion process is:

```text
       ↓

       ↓

       ↓

       ↓

       ↓

       ↓

```

## ⚙️ Core Hardware Mechanism

### 1. Speed Breaker Platform

The upper platform responds to the downward force produced by a passing vehicle.

### 2. Linear Rack & Pinion

The downward movement of the platform moves a rack, which engages with a pinion gear and converts linear motion into rotational motion.

### 3. Return Springs

Spring-loaded assemblies return the platform and rack to their original position after a vehicle passes, allowing the system to operate repeatedly.

### 4. Generator / Dynamo

The rotational movement generated by the gear mechanism drives a low-RPM dynamo, converting mechanical energy into electrical energy through electromagnetic induction.

### 5. Power Management

The generated electrical output is processed using:

* Rectifiers
* Voltage regulators
* Batteries
* Supercapacitors
* Power-conditioning circuitry

This helps stabilize and store the generated electricity.

### 6. Smart Monitoring

The system is designed for integration with IoT monitoring to track system operation, traffic activity, and maintenance-related parameters.

## 🧠 Software & IoT

PulseRoads is designed to combine the physical energy-harvesting mechanism with a smart monitoring layer.

The software platform can be used to monitor:

* ⚡ Energy generation
* 🚗 Vehicle/traffic activity
* 🌡️ Road and system conditions
* 🔋 Stored energy
* ⚙️ Hardware status
* 🛠️ Maintenance requirements

The collected data can support **predictive maintenance** and help identify potential mechanical or alignment issues.
